# Hero sequence audit

Observed the running Form → Behavior → Convergence → Product sequence at slow, fast and reverse scroll, with the Behavior trigger and Signal Composer.

The original Behavior packet is a small sphere that terminates inside a static lane; it does not drive the following shot. At the chapter boundary, a separate particle halo, stack of rectangles and wireframe node enter as three small silhouettes. Their placement and spring movement make the metaphor read as three Three.js props approaching each other. The camera backs away to observe rather than acquiring the signal and revealing the scale of an existing world. Segment tubes, repeated rectangles, octahedron nodes and point sprites retain a demo-like grammar. The impact is an outward ring, light spike and debris; Product is already modeled as a ring and six straight bars and fades into view before the energy has resolved. There is no shared field, sustained cross-system reaction, pre-impact suspension, inward event, visual silence or progressive material formation. Audio has only short oscillator cues. The primary issue is narrative causality and silhouette, not easing or particle count.

Keep the raw-scroll/smoothed-playhead split, chapter semantics, opt-in sound, adaptive quality, reduced-motion mode, and the earlier Attention/Form architecture. Replace the three-object collision and rack-shaped final object. Rebuild the Behavior exit, hero camera path, shared convergence field, implosion, aftermath and spatial Product as one causal sequence. Judge the result in motion in desktop and mobile browsers after implementation.

## Result and review

The former three-body convergence, outward collision and six-bar product were removed. The final Behavior packet now continues into a curved signal with measured velocity, a persistent ribbon and local light. A damped camera rig picks it up and exits the network into a larger matter field, discontinuous spatial lattice and energetic conduits. The attraction and vortex terms of one convergence field drive particle velocity; the same center and field strength bend structural vertices and energy paths in shaders. The event suspends the playhead briefly, draws the systems inward, removes almost all visual energy, then resolves residual particles into an asymmetric surface and its routes. The Product panel appears when actual formation passes its reveal threshold, including after a paused or slow frame.

Visual checks covered desktop and narrow mobile framing, chapter jumps, a Behavior signal, Product routing, reverse navigation, calm mode and console errors. Mobile uses fewer particles, smaller architectural spans and a separate camera distance. No bloom, chromatic aberration or full-screen postprocessing was added. The matter field uses CPU velocity integration with a dynamic GPU point buffer; a texture-based GPU simulation remains a possible upgrade if future profiling shows the CPU field is the limiting cost. In-app browser capture throttles to roughly one frame per second, so the motion solver catches up from long frames while particle integration stays substepped. The user-facing production page should also be checked on a full-rate device after deployment.
