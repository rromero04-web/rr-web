# CONVERGENCE — cinematic redesign

The interactive experience now uses four distinct visual laws rather than variations of one machined object.

- **Attention:** 1,900–5,200 GPU particles flow through three spatial currents. The camera begins inside the field, then reveals it; pointer position displaces nearby particles.
- **Form:** the same particles quantize into rows while an architectural grid, planes, measurements and typography lock into place. A lateral camera move exposes the depth of the construction.
- **Behavior:** cold seven-lane routes replace the static grid. Packets propagate across switching nodes automatically and in response to the test-signal button.
- **Convergence:** a wide shot reveals the warm field, violet spatial frame and cyan network as separate bodies. They orbit, pause, collide at scroll progress ~0.76, and release an expanding ring and fragments. The collision is authored as a deterministic scroll-driven simulation so reversing or seeking the page yields the same frame.
- **Product:** the result is a floating circular information sculpture with warm intake, spatial hierarchy and a cold output path. Selection and routing alter its state.

The WebGL scene is decorative to assistive technology. Reading mode now contains matching static illustrations, all narrative copy and the functional signal composer. Reduced motion and forced colors continue to use reading mode. Quality tiers adjust particle count and pixel ratio, and the performance governor steps down quality when frame time stays high.

The poster and social image are generated with `node scripts/render-convergence-art.mjs` from the same warm/violet/cold composition.
